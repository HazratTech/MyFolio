# RelayWorks Backlink Plan

## Positioning to use everywhere

RelayWorks builds custom website AI chatbots, WhatsApp lead workflows, and CRM-connected automation for service businesses. Founder-led delivery by Hazrat Ummar Shaikh.

Use the same business name, site URL, founder name, services, and social profiles in every listing. Link the relevant service page, not only the home page.

## Start here

1. Clutch: Create a free company profile under AI development, chatbot development, and custom software development. Add 2 to 3 real client references and one detailed case study. Do not buy reviews.
   - https://clutch.co/tools
   - Target link: https://relayworks.dev/ai-chatbot-development

2. GoodFirms: Apply for the free listing, then complete the portfolio, services, pricing range, and client-review sections. Choose chatbot development and software development only when they reflect real work.
   - https://www.goodfirms.co/get-listed
   - Target link: https://relayworks.dev/ai-chatbot-development

3. TechBehemoths: Create or claim the agency listing using the relayworks.dev email address. Add an honest portfolio, location, service focus, and contact details.
   - https://techbehemoths.com/companies/get-listed
   - Target link: https://relayworks.dev/services

4. DesignRush: Submit an agency profile with a real project image and short description. Keep the category focused on AI development or software development rather than adding every possible service.
   - https://www.designrush.com/
   - Target link: https://relayworks.dev/ai-chatbot-development

## Earned links that matter more than directories

1. Ask three satisfied clients for a small public case study on their company blog or project announcement. Give them a draft covering the problem, implementation, and result, but let them publish in their own words.

2. Publish one useful implementation guide each month, based on work you can prove. Good first topics:
   - How a service business should design human handoff in a website AI chatbot
   - WhatsApp lead qualification: questions to ask before a sales call
   - CRM handoff patterns for small service teams

3. Contribute a technical answer, workshop, or short guest article to communities where business operators already learn about automation. Only include a contextual RelayWorks link when it genuinely supports the answer.

4. Keep GitHub, LinkedIn, and other founder profiles linked to relayworks.dev with a consistent description. Add a pinned repository or case-study README when there is public code to show.

## Rules

- Do not buy bulk backlinks, PBN links, comment links, or “500 directory submissions.”
- Do not use exact-match anchor text repeatedly. Mix `RelayWorks`, `AI chatbot development`, the page URL, and natural sentence links.
- Do not publish invented results, client names, ratings, or screenshots.
- Record every acquired link, target page, date, and referral traffic in a sheet. Keep the sources that send qualified visitors; ignore vanity metrics.

## First 30 days

Week 1: Complete Clutch, GoodFirms, and TechBehemoths profiles.

Week 2: Ask three real past clients for reviews or a short case study.

Week 3: Publish one detailed chatbot handoff guide and share it from the founder LinkedIn profile.

Week 4: Review Google Search Console impressions, which queries trigger the AI chatbot page, referral sessions from listings, and completed lead events.
