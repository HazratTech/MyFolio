# Solo Agency Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Playbook

This skill provides tactical playbooks for solo software engineers operating boutique agencies (like RelayWorks) competing for small-to-midsize client budgets ($500 – $3,000).

## 1. The "Missing Platform Proof" Playbook (Handling the iOS Objection)

When a developer has shipped products on Android (Google Play) but lacks a live consumer app on the Apple App Store:

### The Deadly Mistake
- Pretending to be an Apple veteran without proof, or placing a broken App Store badge.
- Ignoring the client's fear (clients will assume you cannot build iOS).

### The Winning Soloist Strategy
1. **Radical Transparency**: State honestly that your past client apps were private internal tools or Android-first MVPs.
2. **KMP Architectural Leverage**: Explain that 75–80% of the app's brain (networking, local database, offline queue, business logic) is written in pure shared Kotlin. The iOS layer is pure, clean **SwiftUI** with zero artificial webview lag.
3. **The Apple TestFlight Milestone**: Deliver testable iOS builds directly to the client's iPhone via Apple TestFlight before any final payment.
4. **100% App Store Approval Guarantee**: Include a contract clause: *"If Apple rejects the app due to code or guideline issues, I resolve every reviewer note at zero additional cost until approved."*
